There's a picture hidden in this grid, made just for you. Fill in the right squares to find out what it is.

How it works

The numbers next to each row and above each column say how many filled squares are in that line, in order. 3 1 means a run of 3 filled squares, then at least one gap, then 1 more.

Tools, tips and settings

Tools

  • Fill paints squares. Tap a filled square with Fill again to clear it.
  • Cross marks squares you're sure are empty. Tap a cross with Cross again to remove it.
  • A drag changes every square in its path: Cross turns filled squares into crosses, and Fill turns crosses into filled squares. Start a drag on a square that already has that mark to clear the marks instead.
  • Drag along a row or column to paint lots of squares at once. A pink bubble counts how many squares the line covers.
  • Numbers cross out on their own when their run is finished, and you can tap any number to cross it out yourself.
  • When a row or column matches its clue, its empty squares get light crosses. Change that line again and they disappear.
  • Hint solves one row or column for you. If something's wrong, it fixes that first.
  • Check shows any wrong squares in red.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom: the numbers grow with the board. Drag with two fingers, or one finger on the numbers, to move around. Out and In zoom too.
  • The numbers scroll with the board. To keep them on screen while you move around, tap the small tabs in the top-left corner: ▸ for the row numbers, ▾ for the column numbers. Tap again (🔒) to let them scroll away.

Tips

  • Start with the big numbers. A 15 in a line of 20 always covers the middle 10 squares.
  • The little picture in the top corner shows your whole grid, with a pink frame around the part on screen. Tap it to see the whole board, and tap again to jump back.
  • Your progress saves by itself, so you can stop and come back any time.
